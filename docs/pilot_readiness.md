# AnnaSetu AI (अन्नसेतु) — Pilot Readiness & Operational Runbook

## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
This document establishes the verified operational framework for launching the initial **AnnaSetu AI Supervised Pilot**. The pilot connects high-volume commercial food donors (banquet halls, caterers, corporate cafeterias) with verified recipient organizations (orphanages, elderly care ashrams, community kitchens) under strict compliance with the **FSSAI Surplus Food Regulations (2019)**.

---

## 2. Pilot Cohort & Geographic Scope
- **Location:** Delhi-NCR / Bengaluru Urban Corridor.
- **Participating Donors (3-5 partners):**
  - Aerocity Convention Banquets (Commercial caterer)
  - Grand Regency Banquets (Wedding / event hall)
  - Tech Park Central Cafeteria (Corporate catering)
- **Participating Recipients (5 verified organizations):**

- **Logistics Pool:** 12 trained volunteer drivers equipped with insulated food-grade transport bags and temperature probes.

## 3. Operational Protocols & Food Safety Governance

### 3.1 Strict Evidence Verification Gate
1. **Compulsory Visual Evidence:** Every food listing MUST include high-resolution photographic evidence (overhead tray view, container seal status). Listings without photos are rejected immediately with `MissingEvidenceError`.
2. **Cold/Hot Chain Holding Constraints:**
   - **Hot Food:** Must be maintained $\ge 60^\circ\text{C}$ or delivered within 4 hours of preparation if held at ambient temperature.
   - **Cold Food:** Must be maintained $\le 5^\circ\text{C}$.
   - **Exceeded Duration:** Any cooked perishable food exceeding 4 hours ambient duration is automatically placed **ON_HOLD** and diverted to professional safety reviewers.

### 3.2 Physical Handover Gate & OTP Verification
- **Zero False-Success Rule:** No allocation is counted as rescued food until the recipient organization performs a physical inspection.
- **Physical Inspection Requirements:**
  1. Aroma / Odor check: No sour, rancid, or off-odors.
  2. Visual inspection: Clean, sealed food-grade containers.
  3. Temperature probe spot-check: Hot food $\ge 60^\circ\text{C}$ or ambient cold.
  4. OTP verification: Recipient verifies the 4-digit cryptographically random OTP generated by the coordinator.
- **Rejection Procedure:** If any physical check fails, the recipient enters `visual_inspection_passed = False`. The food is categorized as `REJECTED_AT_HANDOVER`, diverted for compost/bio-methanation, and **ZERO kilograms are credited to impact metrics**.

## 4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
| Metric | Pilot Target | Measurement Method |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Time-to-Match** | $< 15\text{ minutes}$ | Timestamp from listing publication to recipient acceptance |
| **Inspection Pass Rate** | $\ge 95\%$ | Ratio of `VERIFIED_DELIVERED` to scheduled handovers |
| **Pickup-to-Delivery Window** | $< 45\text{ minutes}$ | Volunteer driver transit tracking |
| **Zero False Claims** | $100\%$ | Audit reconciliation between delivered kg and impact totals |
| **Food Safety Incidents** | $0$ | Recipient post-consumption health check follow-ups |

---

## 5. Authoritative Impact Estimation Standards
- **Standard Meal Portion:** $0.35\text{ kg}$ ($350\text{ g}$) of wholesome cooked food per individual meal (FSSAI baseline).
- **Greenhouse Gas Avoidance:** $2.5\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e}$ mitigated per $1.0\text{ kg}$ of food diverted from municipal landfills.
- **Virtual Water Conservation:** $800\text{ liters}$ of embedded agricultural water saved per $1.0\text{ kg}$ of rescued meal.
